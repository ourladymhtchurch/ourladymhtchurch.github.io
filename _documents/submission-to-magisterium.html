Deo gratias...+ On February 2, 2026, the Feast of the Purification of the Blessed Virgin Mary, I wrote to Father Dominic Crawford to inform him that neither Our Lady of the Most Holy Trinity Church nor I could accept his requirements and conditions that he proposed to us in November 2025, that we reject Baptism of Desire, Baptism of Blood, and Periodic Continence by name. I also informed him that I have renounced my heretical errors against these Catholic doctrines, and thus submit myself to the Magisterium of the Catholic Church, in humility and in imitation of the most profound humility of Our Blessed Mother the Most Blessed Ever-Virgin Mary Our Queen and also in imitation of her most loving submission and obedience to the entirety of God’s Most Holy Law. In addition, I encouraged him to do the same. 

Therefore, I pray for the conversion of Father Dominic Crawford, and will continue to pray for him, and I humbly ask that you also please pray for the conversion of his soul, the souls of his family members, the souls of his chapel, and  the souls of those former members of Our Lady of the Most Trinity Church who believe his errors and heresies against the Magisterium of the Catholic Church along with his schism against the Unity of Faith of the Catholic Church.
