About Us

Our Lady of the Most Holy Trinity Chapel

We were chartered as a Traditional Roman Catholic Church with our legal name of Our Lady of the Most Holy Trinity Church in the state of Texas on May 13, 1993; however, on July 7, 2026, we received approval from the Secretary of State of Texas to use our assumed name Our Lady of the Most Holy Trinity Chapel. We are not part of the Vatican II Church.  We are thankful to God's divine Providence that we were previously under the authority of Bishop Martin Davila Gandara of the Sociedad Sacerdotal Trento (SST) until on October 20, 2026, when he and Father Lamberto Melchor Moreno, SST notified us that they deeply regret that they will not be able to assist us further due to health issues of several of their priests. We are very grateful to have had the Priestly Ministry of the SST at our Chapel in Corpus Christi, which began with the first visit of Father Damaso Ruiz Loya (RIP) in June 2009 for about two to three weeks each visit several times a year until December 2013 before his death on February 21, 2014 in Mexico. Further, we are thankful to have had the help of other SST priests since then, plus the retired priest, Father John Akito Nariai (RIP) who died in Tokyo in January, 2020. We will keep them all in our prayers.


Therefore, after praying and seeking to find other Catholic clergy to minister to us,  our daily Rosaries and prayers were answered on Oct. 22, 2026, when His Excellency Bishop Mark Anthony Pivarunas, CMRI graciously accepted to help us with Catholic priests beginning on the great Feast of Christ the King on Oct. 25th, and we are most grateful to him! Deo gratias...+


We profess all the dogmas and doctrines of the Catholic Church, as taught by the true Popes until the death of Pope Pius XII in 1958 before the New Church or Vatican II Church was established.  We pray for a true and holy Pope soon, as we pray and encourage others to pray the daily Rosary and wear the the Brown Scapular:  a garment of grace for the salvation of souls in the Catholic Church of our Lord Jesus Christ reigning through true devotion to the Immaculate Heart of Mary and Saint Joseph...+  


"One Lord, one faith, one baptism" [Ephesians 4:5]
