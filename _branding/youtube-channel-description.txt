Welcome to the YouTube channel of Our Lady of the Most Holy Trinity Chapel, a Traditional Roman Catholic Chapel in Corpus Christi, Texas, offering the Traditional Holy Latin Mass.

Here you will find sermons preached at the Chapel by the priests of the Congregation of Mary Immaculate Queen (CMRI), who serve us through the kindness of His Excellency Bishop Mark A. Pivarunas, CMRI, along with other sermons, lectures and devotions for the salvation of souls.

We profess all the dogmas and doctrines of the Catholic Church, as taught by the true Popes until the death of Pope Pius XII in 1958. We pray for a true and holy Pope, and we encourage everyone to pray the daily Rosary and wear the Brown Scapular.

All are welcome. For the Holy Latin Mass schedule, the Dress Code and more sermons, please visit our website.
